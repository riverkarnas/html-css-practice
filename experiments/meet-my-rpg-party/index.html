Slimes & Soups

D&D 5 | Ongoing

An old-aged cook meets a runaway monk tiefling and they set foot to explore the world - and its recipes. On their journey, they meet a deadly untrusting elf. They get into a fight with skeletons, and the local taverner is turned into a book. A teenage goblin shows up. What could go wrong?

Meet the party

Azhara

Monk · Tiefling

Left as a baby at a monastary, Azhara grew up training and studying amongst monks who made her believe her half-demon existence was a curse to be "purified" with hard work. After discovering in hidden books her tiefling existence might be more than what was taught, she took the first opportunity to run away from the monastary and explore the world with Mrs. Mirabel.

Boblin

Druid · Goblin

Boblin grew up in the mountains, mentored by the local shaman - with whom he learned about herbs and how to brew potions. However, a couple years ago the mineral exploitation of the mountains started, and the shaman passed away due to respiratory issues. Boblin then found out he had an uncle in a nearby city, so then he set foot to meet his remaining family. He lived a quiet life, one that helped him through his grief, until the local taverner was turned into a book.
